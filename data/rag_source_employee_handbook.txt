NORTHSTAR ANALYTICS — EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK EXCERPT
Version 1.0 | January 2026

1. PURPOSE
Northstar Analytics provides data products for retail businesses. This handbook explains selected workplace policies for full-time employees.

2. WORKING HOURS
Core collaboration hours are Monday through Friday, 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM in the employee's local time zone. Full-time employees are expected to work 40 hours per week. Teams may agree on flexible schedules with their manager when core collaboration hours are covered.

3. REMOTE WORK
Eligible full-time employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Employees must agree on their remote-work schedule with their manager and remain reachable during core collaboration hours. New employees become eligible after completing 30 calendar days of employment.

4. PAID TIME OFF
Full-time employees receive 24 paid time off (PTO) days in each calendar year. Up to 6 unused PTO days may be carried into the next calendar year. Any remaining unused PTO above 6 days expires on December 31. PTO requests for five or more consecutive workdays should be submitted at least 14 days before the first requested day.

5. SICK LEAVE
Employees receive 10 paid sick-leave days each calendar year. Sick leave is separate from PTO and does not reduce an employee's PTO balance. A medical certificate may be requested for an absence lasting more than three consecutive workdays.

6. PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT
Each full-time employee can claim up to USD 1,200 per calendar year for approved professional-development courses, books, or conferences. The employee must obtain written manager approval before making a purchase. Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days after the event or purchase.

7. EQUIPMENT
Northstar provides a laptop and one external monitor to each full-time employee. Employees working remotely more than two days per week may request a company-approved ergonomic chair, subject to manager approval.

8. INFORMATION SECURITY
Employees must use multi-factor authentication for company systems and must not share passwords. Customer data may be accessed only through approved company tools. Suspected security incidents must be reported to security@northstar.example within one hour of discovery.

9. QUESTIONS ABOUT THIS HANDBOOK
For policy clarification, contact the People Operations team through the internal help portal.
